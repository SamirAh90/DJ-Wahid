# DJ Wahid SEO copy and search themes

The source material is DJ Wahid's website and the event captions supplied by the
owner. Past wedding, graduation, family-party and concert posts describe experience;
they are not advertised as upcoming events. Booking contact details remain DJ
Wahid's own. Couples' names and other organisers' phone numbers are not used.

## Page titles and descriptions

English title: **DJ Wahid | Afghan & Persian Wedding DJ in Malmö**

English description: Afghan, Persian and Kurdish wedding DJ based in Malmö.
International music for weddings, birthdays, graduation parties and corporate
events. Book DJ Wahid.

Swedish title: **DJ Wahid | Afghansk & persisk bröllops-DJ i Malmö**

Swedish description: Boka DJ Wahid i Malmö – afghansk, persisk och kurdisk
bröllops-DJ. Musik till bröllop, studentfester, födelsedagar och företagsevent.

## Implementation and editing

- Titles and descriptions appear in HTML metadata and social sharing metadata.
- The first hero headline and biography contain relevant, visible service copy.
- STRATO serves English and Swedish initial HTML at `/?lang=en` and `/?lang=sv`.
- Canonical and language-alternate URLs use the real HTTPS `origin` in the private
  server configuration. Localhost and the example domain do not receive them.
- ProfessionalService structured data describes DJ Wahid, Malmö, Sweden and the
  existing contact details. No ratings or future events are invented.
- Admin → Website → Search engine settings edits both languages. Biography and
  hero copy remain editable through Text and translations.

Titles and descriptions use natural wording following
[Google Search Central's title guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link)
and [description guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/snippet).
This work updates the local site; public indexing requires deployment to the
configured domain. Search engines choose their own result titles and snippets.
