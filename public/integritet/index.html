Data and purposes

When you contact us by phone or social media, we receive information you provide, such as your name, contact details and event information. We use this to respond to your enquiry and plan a possible assignment. Please do not send sensitive personal data in messages.

For booking and rental enquiries, processing is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract and to perform an agreed contract. For other enquiries and website protection, processing is based on our legitimate interests in communicating with people who contact us and maintaining secure operations. Data that must be retained by law is processed to meet legal obligations.

The current booking form only displays a local confirmation in your browser. It does not send a booking request or store form submissions in the server database. Use the phone number or contact links to make an actual booking. The website does not use profiling or automated decision-making.

Recipients and hosting

STRATO is the hosting provider for the planned published website and supplies the technical environment for its files and database. During visits, the host may process IP addresses, timestamps, requested URLs, browser and device information for delivery, troubleshooting and security. The local preview runs on the development computer.

Fonts, images, music and videos are served from the website's own files. Fonts are not requested from Google Fonts during your visit. If you contact us through an external service, that provider also processes information under its own terms. Data may be shared with providers who need it to operate the service, where required by law or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Retention and security

Information from enquiries is kept for as long as necessary to handle the enquiry, perform an assignment and meet legal obligations or address legal claims. Administrator accounts and published content remain in the database until removed when no longer needed. Hosting logs and backups are managed according to the hosting provider's agreements and procedures.

Access should be limited to authorised people and providers who need the information. The website is configured to use HTTPS on the published domain. Administrator passwords are stored as password hashes. Login cookies use HttpOnly and SameSite protection, with Secure enabled when HTTPS is used.

Cookies and local storage

Background music starts with sound enabled on each visit, subject to your browser's autoplay rules. You can mute or unmute it using the music control. Your mute choice applies to the current page visit and is not saved in local storage.

The website stores your chosen language under dj-language, so English or Swedish remains selected when you return. You can remove this preference by clearing website data in your browser. Music and videos are loaded from the website's own files.

Administrator functions use dj_session for login and dj_csrf to protect against forged requests. The CSRF cookie lasts up to 30 days. The login cookie's lifetime depends on the server configuration, normally 30 days, and it is removed on logout. You can block or delete cookies in your browser, but administrator functions may then stop working.

The current website has no analytics tools or advertising pixels. If new features or storage are introduced, this information will be updated and consent requirements reviewed.

Administrator accounts and technical operations

Administrator account details and administrator-created content are stored in the website database. The server may process technical information, including IP addresses and request times, for operation and troubleshooting.

External links

Links to Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, WhatsApp and other websites lead to external services. Their own privacy policies apply when you open them.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may request access to your personal data, correction of inaccurate information, erasure or restriction of processing. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. In certain cases, you may receive your data in a structured format to transfer it to another provider, known as data portability.

Contact Wahid Jafari at 0046736402445 or via WhatsApp. Describe the contact or processing your request concerns. We may need to verify your identity before disclosing or changing information. Some data may need to be retained to meet legal obligations.

If you believe your data is being handled incorrectly, you may lodge a complaint with the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).
